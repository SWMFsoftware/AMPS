# I22 atomic photoionization pilot — test contract

Decision 2, part A of the [M3 follow-up](../../M3_COMPLETION_FOLLOWUP_20261124.md),
approved 2026-11-26 (UTC). The record is
[reference/atomic_pilot_approval_20261126.json](reference/atomic_pilot_approval_20261126.json),
including two same-day revisions made before any run, plus two approved
revision records written after runs (listed below). The protocol that every
case binds as transport-policy evidence is
[reference/atomic_pilot_protocol.json](reference/atomic_pilot_protocol.json).
This is a supporting development pilot; formal I22 stays **SKIPPED / NOT QUALIFIED**.

## ID / name

I22 (existing ID, no new ID): atomic photoionization pilot for He, Ne and Ar.

## Purpose

Run He, Ne and Ar photoionization X → X⁺ + e⁻ as real particle events
through the normal native cohort owner. Then verify the event times, the
conserved ledger, the D04 rate binding and the MPI/refinement behaviour
against independent oracles.

## Production capability and entry points

- `Moon::Species::PhysicalReactionEvents::AtomicPhotoionization` (formerly
  `SodiumPhotoionization`, which remains an alias): one provider for 23Na,
  4He, 20Ne and 40Ar. The parent state's nucleus selects the target. Its D04
  family must be the one complete reactive X → X⁺ + e channel. He, Ne and Ar
  are admitted only under the source-effective-photon convention.
- `Moon::Chemistry::SodiumCohort::Prepared` (normal startup binding) and the
  native adapter `srcMoon/test/sodium_cohort/native.cpp`, which now records
  `source_parent` in each input receipt.
- Normal Moon startup (`main_lib.cpp`, `MoonInput.cpp`): explicit
  `neon_*`/`argon_*` reference-weight and ion-speed keys, following He⁺.
  This also fixes the old Ne⁺ weight copy, which copied the Ne weight onto
  Ne and left Ne⁺ unset.

## Configuration

Zero-acceleration compiled builds with the Na weight reference, the tracked
parent and its ground ion: Na/He/He_PLUS, Na/Ne/Ne_plus and Na/Ar/AR_PLUS,
built by `build_m3_source_qualification.py --reference-problem
homogeneous-zero-acceleration`. The decks and case controls are
[reference/atomic_pilot/](reference/atomic_pilot/), generated by
`prepare_atomic_pilot_cases.py` (use `--check` for byte equality). They are
derived from the I21 compact sphere deck and change only the approved fields.

| Case | Horizon [s] | Parents | Expected events (1 AU rate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| He-quiet | 65,536 | 8,192 | ≈30 |
| He-active | 65,536 | 8,192 | ≈90 |
| Ne-quiet | 262,144 | 2,048 | ≈96 |
| Ne-active | 262,144 | 512 | ≈73 |
| Ar-quiet | 1,048,576 | 256 | ≈77 |
| Ar-active | 1,048,576 | 128 | ≈71 |

He-quiet was approved at 16,384 parents. The first run with that count did not
finish one mover run within the existing one-hour owner limit: the sixth
launch was stopped (ERROR, retained), and the 8-rank launches were no faster
than the 1-rank ones. The user then chose 8,192 parents
([revision record](reference/atomic_pilot_revision_20261126.json)). No
criterion or limit changed.

Campaign-2 (all six cases, both movers) passed every He check. In Ne and Ar
only the strict-decrease refinement check failed: the event-time error stayed
flat at about 1.5×10⁻¹⁵ of the horizon at 8, 16 and 32 steps. The decks had
inherited `tracing_maximum_substep_s = 8192` from the I21 deck, and production
builds the photon history over each mover substep, interpolating (AU/r)²
linearly. Every Ne and Ar step is at least 8,192 s, so step refinement left
the exposure grid unchanged. The independent prediction for 8,192 s,
h²·6(v/r)²/12 = 1.56×10⁻¹⁵ (v = 1 m/s, r = 1.4689×10¹¹ m), matches the
plateau. The user chose to set the cap to the coarsest common step in Ne and
Ar and re-run them
([revision b](reference/atomic_pilot_revision_20261126b.json)). The
campaign-2 FAILs and their inputs are retained. No criterion or limit changed.

Common settings: start (10⁷, 0, 0) m in frozen LSO, +1 m/s along x, SPICE
Sun at 2009-01-24T00:00:00, sphere Moon, Earth occultation off, seed 731993,
explicit reference weights 7:7, represented number 2.625 per parent, both
force-cut-surface movers, 8/16/32 common steps, and 1 and 8 MPI ranks with
OMP 1. In every case the mover substep cap `tracing_maximum_substep_s` equals
the coarsest common step (horizon/8: 8,192 s He, 32,768 s Ne, 131,072 s Ar),
so each refinement level also refines the photon-history segments. The model-error limits are 1e-8, 1e-8 and 1e-4, as P02. The
classical-speed cap is 3.4×10⁶ m/s: the Ne-active photoelectron reaches
about 3.13×10⁶ m/s, and the relativistic kinetic error at the cap is about
9.6×10⁻⁵.

The horizons keep every recoiling ion inside the AMPS domain, which spans
x ∈ [−7.59×10⁸, +5.21×10⁷] m and |y|, |z| ≤ 6.76×10⁷ m. The worst-case ion
stays at least 1.1×10⁷ m from the boundary; the smallest margin is Ar-active,
at 1.17×10⁷ m. The cohort ledger has no domain-exit category.

## Inputs, data and hashes

- Atomic-family state profile `test_output/srcMoon/m3-followup-MfThQfny/atomic-family/profile.json`
  (`42943c73…`). It supplies the ground-ion references from CODATA 2018 (4He),
  Eikema et al. 1994 (20Ne) and Velchev et al. 1999 (40Ar), plus NIST
  isotope masses.
- D04 PHIDRATES `thresholds.csv` (`a3335b3b…`) and `photo_rates_1au.csv`
  (`3aa54915…`); package QA must be PASS and the bytes must match provenance.
- `prepare_atomic_pilot_inputs.py` writes the three eight-column crosswalks
  and a derived profile. The profile lists them in
  `atomic_photoionization_crosswalks`, beside the unchanged Na
  `atomic_extension`. The case preparer accepts only a crosswalk listed under
  its own parent.

## Independent references (oracles)

1. Exact source-bound event-ledger reconstruction (`verify_ledger.py`): the
   number, charge, mass, momentum and energy of each event, and the D04
   rate, threshold and mean excess of the cohort's own parent family.
2. Straight-line integrated-exposure event-time oracle
   (`illuminated-zero-force-lso-x-axis-v1`, `verify_numerical.py`). It uses
   independently reproduced HMAC source clocks.
3. The existing I21 inverse-square criterion (0.1 %), applied to the shared
   exposure service as a supporting check.
4. Original-parent identity between the 1- and 8-rank runs at each level.
5. Verifier-side target table (`sodium_cohort/atomic_targets.py`), written
   independently of the C++ provider.

## Units, frames and time

SI throughout. Positions and velocities are in frozen LSO metres; the
inertial frame is SUN-origin J2000. Time is physical seconds from the
transport origin, and the geometry basis epoch is frozen.

## Deterministic and stochastic controls

HMAC-SHA256 keyed draws (seed 731993, cohort ID, parent, purpose, index).
Statistics: the 2 % ensemble rate gate is reported, not scored, at these
cohort sizes. Bitwise identity is not required.

## Procedure

`prepare_physical_case.py` → one-rank reference receipt
(`run_m3_sodium_cohort.py --mode run`) →
`I21/prepare_cohort.py --test-id I22` → `run_all_test.py --only I22
--i22-cohort-plan PLAN --i22-cohort-plan-sha256 SHA` → independent audit
(`sodium_cohort/audit.py`, which the executor starts).

## Metrics and acceptance criteria (unchanged)

- Event-time refinement: the maximum normalized first-hit error decreases
  with each refinement, and the finest level is ≤ 0.002 (the
  `NUMERICAL_LIMIT['I22']` approved addendum).
- Ledger: exact closure within the declared arithmetic bounds, and the D04
  endpoint values match exactly.
- Inverse square: relative error ≤ 0.001.
- MPI: identical original-parent outcomes and event times at 1 and 8 ranks.
- The ensemble rate, compared with the oracle survival interval, is reported
  only.

Expected PASS result: every check above passes in all six cases with both
movers.

## Expected failure modes

An ion deleted at the domain boundary (an ERROR in the ledger or live
readback). A product above the speed cap (production OutsideDomain, so
ERROR). A changed source or crosswalk (ERROR). An absent profile, D04 or
producer (SKIPPED). An event inside the shadow, or a non-decreasing
refinement (FAIL). A substep cap below the coarsest common step leaves the
exposure grid unrefined and produces a flat refinement error (FAIL, as in
campaign-2 Ne/Ar); the generator and `test_m3_i22_atomic_pilot.py` refuse it.

## Predecessor gates

Formal I22 needs U15, U24 and the same-campaign I13–I20. Under the approved
M3 development exception these are recorded and do not block. They cannot
be credited.

## Artifacts

Each run keeps `result.json`, `run_manifest.json`, stdout/stderr, input
snapshots, native receipts, per-rank event ledgers and parent records. It
also keeps audit JSON/CSV/figures, producer build manifests with source
identity, and the build logs.

## Status semantics

PASS / FAIL / ERROR / SKIPPED with exits 0 / 1 / 2 / 77. A pilot PASS is
supporting evidence only. It does not cover molecular chemistry, charged
breakup, U24 or observational agreement.
