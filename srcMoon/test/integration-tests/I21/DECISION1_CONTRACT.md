# I21 decision 1: varying exposure, geometry and weight controls

The user approved decision 1 of the
[M3 completion follow-up](../../M3_COMPLETION_FOLLOWUP_20261124.md) on
2026-11-26, then approved the case design below the same day
([approval record](reference/decision1_approval_20261126.json)). These are
supporting development cases of the existing I21 owner. Formal I21 keeps its
predecessors and remains **SKIPPED / NOT QUALIFIED**. No test ID or threshold
changes.

## Rules recorded by the approval

* **Constant cohort.** The constant-hazard cohort's strict timing-refinement
  FAIL stays visible in its original artifacts. Its errors (about 5e-17 of the
  horizon) sit at the binary64 floor and cannot decrease further. Changing
  its role in the formal case design needs a separate review of the contract
  and the evidence.
* **Varying rate (item 2).** The existing pair satisfies it: the standalone
  radial fixture ([contract](VARYING_EXPOSURE_CONTRACT.md); production
  quadrature and clock; errors 7.7e-4, 2.0e-4, 5.0e-5) and the linked SPICE
  straight line (full PIC path; errors 1.0e-10, 2.5e-11, 6.1e-12). A strongly
  varying linked case is infeasible: with the D04 normalization and a frozen
  static Sun, doubling the Sun distance within one optical depth needs a start
  150–1,700 AU away or a 10¹⁰–10¹² s horizon. A source near the Moon is
  refused within one solar radius.

## Production changes

* `FrozenProductionField::CreatePrescribedSunAtEpoch` and the sodium cohort
  request fields `prescribedSun`, `prescribedSunPositionM` and
  `prescribedSunIdentity`: verification only. The normal path, SPICE Sun and
  Earth occultation are unchanged when they are absent. A prescribed Sun
  refuses Earth occultation and keeps the one-solar-radius check. The
  controller's exposure identity names the fixture.
* `Moon::LunarPointMassReferenceAcceleration`: the production
  `OrbitalDynamics::AddLunarPointMassAcceleration` term alone, selected only by
  the compile recipe's named reference problem `lunar-point-mass-gravity`
  (marker `_MOON_COHORT_LUNAR_GRAVITY_REFERENCE_`). The normal Moon force is
  unchanged.

## Test-side changes

The native adapter parses `photon_source_geometry`,
`prescribed_sun_identity` and `prescribed_sun_position_lso_m_*`, accepts the
case kind `trajectory-lunar-gravity` only with the exact gravity macro, and
records the production lunar GM. `prepare_cohort.py` selects the oracle
(`--trajectory-oracle`) and binds a prescribed Sun to a reference receipt
produced with the same Sun. `verify_numerical.py` adds the shadow and Kepler
oracles (private mpmath contexts) and the per-level position series.
`compare_distance_endpoints.py` scores the 1 AU / 2 AU ratio from the native
event rates. `test_m3_i21_decision1.py` (U16 battery) checks the oracles
against direct integration and an RK4 orbit, the case generator and the
gravity recipe.

## Status semantics

A missing source, producer or interpreter package gives SKIPPED; a corrupt
input or launch failure gives ERROR; a measured mismatch gives FAIL. A
supporting PASS leaves formal I21 SKIPPED.
