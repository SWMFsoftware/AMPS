# Proton–sodium ground-product capture at 4 keV/u

## What the database can establish

The proposed reaction is a proton capturing the valence electron of ground-state Na(3s) into H(1s), leaving the closed-shell Na⁺ ion. The incident energy is 4 keV per atomic mass unit in the target-rest frame. This source review recovers the publication and figure behind the archived cross section and two published calculations for the selected channel.

The 1987 follow-up by Shingal and Bransden gives **0.4073 Å² = 4.073 × 10⁻¹⁷ cm²** for ground capture in its 63-state calculation. The native Table 1 column is projectile laboratory energy in keV, and its selected row is 4.00 keV. The two calculations share authors and use different basis expansions and energy labels. Their difference provides evidence of sensitivity and supplies no confidence interval. [Shingal and Bransden, Table 1, page 4818](https://doi.org/10.1088/0022-3700/20/18/019)

Knoop’s thesis Fig. 5.6 specifically reports a **10–20% systematic uncertainty** from normalization of its shell-selective measurements. Section 5.2 describes fitting the separate hydrogen peaks and normalizing total one-electron capture against earlier absolute data. This is one correlated uncertainty component for the thesis measurements. A point-specific counting or fit error for its small 4 keV/u ground peak is still needed. It would be incorrect to attach the quoted normalization uncertainty to the A34 calculation or use it to resolve the D119990 state label. [Knoop thesis Chapter 5, Fig. 5.6](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/2855778/c5.pdf)

The v2024.1 full-release header search located exactly one numerical record citing the 2004 Knoop DOI. It found no record citing Shingal’s DOI or the related 2005 Zapukhlyak DOI. At the selected energy the archived value is approximately 288 times the A34 value. The cited experimental figure explains why these quantities cannot be interchanged. Experimental ground-product data with applicable uncertainty, and an angular distribution or a justified recoil bound, remain open for I24. No model was selected or activated, and no figures were digitized.
